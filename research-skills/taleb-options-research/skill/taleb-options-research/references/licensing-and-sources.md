## Third-party exclusion

The cited books, podcast/audio, transcripts, original figures, page images, and other third-party works are not bundled and are not relicensed by this project. Titles, authors, bibliographic facts, source IDs, and locators identify sources; they do not imply endorsement or permission to redistribute the underlying works.

The release registries are compact research indexes and project-authored summaries. They are not substitutes for the sources and must not be used to reconstruct them.

Obtain source material lawfully. Do not use this Skill to download unauthorized copies, bypass access controls, or publish full OCR, long quotations, source images, or audio.

## Verification

Use the public release records when originals are unavailable and disclose that the page was not directly re-checked. Use BYOS only for explicitly supplied local material. Store local hashes, page mappings, OCR, and review notes outside the Skill.

When an exact quotation, formula typography, chart, or edition-sensitive page is material, return to a lawful copy and verify the locator. Keep anomalies unresolved until checked.
